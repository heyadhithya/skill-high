# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students and recent graduates who want practical paid work, plus clients who need focused digital services. The same account can hire and offer work; administrators resolve marketplace problems.

## Product Purpose

Skill-High is a student-first marketplace for finding work, agreeing clear scopes, delivering work, and retaining verified Proof-of-Work after graduation.

## Positioning

It connects a small-work marketplace to a portable record of accepted deliveries, reviews, skills, and reliability instead of treating a completed gig as a disposable transaction.

## Operating Context

People create profiles with skills and availability, publish services or client projects, apply or request work, agree an order, message, submit a delivery, request revisions, accept work, review it, and resolve disputes through an administrator.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The product is a Next.js and FastAPI modular monolith using PostgreSQL. It uses server-side sessions, PostgreSQL search and matching, a local payment simulator, local development email sink, private file storage, and a PostgreSQL-backed worker. No real money, real email delivery, paid service, GPU, Redis, Elasticsearch, LLM, or public deployment is required.

## Brand Commitments

The product is named Skill-High. Its approved direction is a cobalt-and-ink workspace on a paper canvas: a photographic landing page introduces a clear Brief → Delivery → Proof journey, while the separate service and project catalogs stay practical and searchable. It is student-first, clear, practical, and trust-oriented, with Schibsted Grotesk display type and DM Sans body copy.

## Evidence on Hand

The running application, database seed records, API tests, and the original proposal at `/home/adi/Downloads/Skill-High.html` are the available evidence. Product claims and testimonials beyond those sources must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Make the next concrete work action obvious.
- Keep agreements, money states, and proof legible.
- Let newcomers begin without penalizing a lack of history.
- Preserve useful evidence after work is complete.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web interface uses semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, actionable validation, responsive layouts, and readable contrast.
