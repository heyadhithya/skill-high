# Marketplace rebuild

Visitor mode: Operate. Scope: catalog, details, authentication, hiring, orders, profile.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Let a client compare useful services and request a defined piece of work. Let a freelancer find a brief and deliver it through an understandable order history.

OWN-WORLD: White surfaces, near-black text, forest and teal actions, compact Manrope and DM Sans typography, restrained borders, and category photography with honest captions.

STORY: Browse services, read the scope and seller information, sign in when ready, request work, then review the submitted delivery. Keep the same navigation and visual system after authentication.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact wordmark/search/account header, category navigation, a short discovery band, useful filters, and a complete row of four listings with seller names and exact prices visible at 1440 by 900. Mobile stacks content and keeps sign-in and buying controls reachable.

FORM: The user's Fiverr references pin the familiar marketplace composition and override concept seed 92cff03d. Astra's implementation plan in docs/REDESIGN_PLAN.md supplies the approved workflow specification; Luna implements it directly.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
